Name
Padyab Maps

Tagline
Maps, search and turn-by-turn navigation with a clean, card-based interface

Description
Padyab Maps shows maps, finds places and gives turn-by-turn navigation, in an
interface of floating cards and sheets over the map.

- Search for places and addresses right from the map.
- Place cards with directions, saving and nearby search.
- Choose between alternative routes before you start, with time, arrival,
  distance and the main road of each.
- Turn-by-turn navigation with a guidance banner, the next turn, the remaining
  time and distance, and spoken instructions.
- Several map styles. No API keys needed: maps from OpenFreeMap, search from
  Photon, routing from the Valhalla server of FOSSGIS.
- Offline maps, search and routing with OSM Scout Server (installed
  separately, then choose the Offline profile in the menu).

Your position stays on the phone. Searches and route requests are sent to the
online services named above, or to OSM Scout Server when you use it.

Padyab Maps is based on Pure Maps by Rinigus and Osmo Salomaa, with a new
interface. It installs next to Pure Maps and keeps its own settings.
